Provisional 2027 rules & suggested outcomes

A win earns 4 points and a draw 2. Teams earn a bonus point for scoring four or more tries, and another for losing by seven points or fewer. Picking a winner assumes a 5-point margin and 3 – 2 tries; open Details to change the margin and tries. Pools are ranked on points, then points difference, then tries. A check marks a qualifying team: the top two in each pool plus the four best third-place teams. These are provisional 2027 qualification rules.
